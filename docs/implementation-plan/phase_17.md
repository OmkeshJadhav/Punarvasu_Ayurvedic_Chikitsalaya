# Phase 17 — AI Clinical Decision Support

## 1. Phase Objective

Build a secure, privacy-conscious, **doctor-in-the-loop AI Clinical Decision Support** system for Punarvasu.

The AI layer may assist an authorized doctor with:

* summarizing relevant clinical information
* organizing consultation information
* identifying potentially relevant considerations
* suggesting questions for further clinical evaluation
* highlighting missing information
* summarizing patient-provided documents where explicitly supported
* assisting with structured clinical reasoning
* optionally suggesting possible areas for consideration

The AI must **never become the final clinical decision-maker**.

The doctor must remain responsible for:

* diagnosis
* clinical interpretation
* prescription
* treatment plan
* patient communication
* final clinical decisions

---

# 2. Fundamental Safety Principle

The system must implement:

```text id="a1x7m4"
Patient / Clinical Data
        ↓
Authorized Doctor
        ↓
AI Clinical Assistance
        ↓
Suggestion / Summary
        ↓
Doctor Review
        ↓
Doctor Decision
        ↓
Clinical Record / Prescription / Treatment Plan
```

Never:

```text id="q8m3x2"
Patient Data
      ↓
AI
      ↓
Automatic Diagnosis
      ↓
Automatic Prescription
```

---

# 3. AI Is Decision Support, Not Autonomous Clinical Care

AI output must always be presented as assistance.

The UI should communicate this clearly.

Example:

```text id="m7q4x8"
AI Clinical Support

This information is generated by AI and is intended to support,
not replace, professional clinical judgment.

Review all suggestions before making clinical decisions.
```

The exact wording can be improved for UX/legal requirements.

---

# 4. Critical Scope Boundary

Phase 17 builds:

```text id="x3m8q7"
AI Assistance
```

It does NOT build:

```text id="q7m4x2"
Autonomous diagnosis
Autonomous prescribing
Autonomous treatment planning
Autonomous patient messaging
Autonomous appointment decisions
```

---

# 5. Doctor-Only Initial Access

AI clinical functionality should initially be available only to appropriately authorized doctors.

Potentially:

```text id="m8x3q7"
doctor
+
clinical permission
+
authorized patient
→ AI Clinical Support
```

Receptionists must not access clinical AI.

Patients must not access internal clinical AI.

Admins do not automatically receive clinical AI access.

---

# 6. Authorization Architecture

Reuse Phase 08.

The flow must be:

```text id="q4m8x2"
Authenticate
→ Verify doctor role
→ Verify clinical-AI permission
→ Verify practitioner scope
→ Verify patient access
→ Verify clinical-record access
→ Prepare minimum required context
→ AI request
```

Never allow:

```text id="x7m3q8"
POST /api/ai
{
  "patientId": "..."
}
```

to automatically grant access.

---

# 7. Resource-Level Authorization

Role alone is insufficient.

Bad:

```ts id="m4x8q2"
if (user.role === "doctor") {
  callAI(patientId);
}
```

Good:

```text id="q8m3x7"
Doctor
→ authorized practitioner
→ authorized patient
→ authorized clinical record
→ permitted AI action
```

---

# 8. Patient Scope

AI requests must be bound to a specific authorized patient/consultation.

Do not allow a doctor to provide arbitrary patient identifiers and retrieve another patient's data.
